Juvi privacy policy

Juvi is the student and faculty app for your college. This policy explains what the app collects, why it collects it, who else handles it, and how to have your account deleted.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Who is responsible

Your college provides Juvi to you and is responsible for the student, faculty and staff records it holds about you. Juvion builds and operates the Juvi app on your college's behalf. If you have a question about your own records, your college office is the right place to ask.

What the Juvi app collects

Your account and your college

Which college you belong to, whether you are a student, faculty or staff member, the identifier you sign in with (your roll number, employee code or email address), and the preferences you set in the app, such as quiet hours and language.

Your devices

Each device you sign in on is recorded: a device identifier, the name you or your phone gives it, the operating system and its version, the app version, when the device last used Juvi, and a push-notification address so Juvi can reach that device.

Your notifications and notices

What was sent to you, when it was delivered, whether you opened it, and your own state on each notice — received, seen, dismissed, reminded, or acknowledged. If you type a comment when you acknowledge a notice, that comment is stored too. A push notification carries the notice's identifier and the office that sent it, plus the title of a single notice that is not confidential. It never carries the notice's body or attachments, or anyone's name, roll number or deadline.

Which parts of the app you use

The app records which features you open and when, so we can see whether Juvi works. These records hold identifiers and labels only — never the contents of anything you read or write.

Your photo, if you add one

A photo you upload through Juvi is stored with your college record as that person's photo. Location and camera information is stripped from the image before it is stored.

Crash reports

If the released app crashes, a crash report is sent so the fault can be found and fixed. It contains no user identifier and no custom data about you.

What the app does not collect

The Juvi app does not collect your phone number, date of birth, home address, government identifiers, or bank or payment details. It takes no payment, and it contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software. The only identifying detail you give it is your sign-in credential — your roll number, your employee code, or your email address, if your college issues email sign-ins.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in, keep your session secure, and let you see and manage your devices.
  • To deliver the notices and alerts your college sends you, and to record that you saw them.
  • To remember your preferences.
  • To find and fix crashes and faults.
  • To understand which parts of the app are used, so we can improve them.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not show your data to other students, or to any other college.

Your data is shared only with the service providers named below, who process it on your college's instructions and for no purpose of their own.

Deleting your account

You can delete your Juvi account from inside the app, under Settings. It takes effect immediately. You can also request deletion without the app installed, using the public deletion page; a request made there takes effect 7 days later, which gives you time to notice and cancel a request you did not make.

Deletion removes your Juvi account and everything the app holds for it: your signed-in devices, your memberships in Juvi spaces, your notification history, any note you added when acknowledging a notice, your app-activity records, and anything the app stored on your device. Deletion is final and cannot be undone.

Deleting your Juvi account does not delete you from your college. Your admission, academic, attendance, fee and employment records belong to the college and are kept under its own record-keeping obligations, as is your profile photo. Your college also keeps an audit record that a notice was sent to you and was acknowledged. The full list of what is deleted and what is kept is on the deletion page.

Your choices

  • Delete your account, from the app or from the deletion page.
  • Turn notifications off in the app's settings, or in your phone's settings.
  • Sign out a device you no longer use, from the app's device list.
  • Ask your college office to correct your personal details, or to explain what it holds.

Students

Juvi is provided to you through your college, and it is intended for college students and staff. If you are a student and your college requires a parent's or guardian's consent for your use of college systems, that is your college's process and your college office can tell you about it.

How we protect it

Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, never as text. Files kept on your college's behalf are encrypted at rest, and location information is removed from photos before they are stored. Access to your data inside your college follows the roles your college has set.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes, and your college will tell you through Juvi or its usual channels.
