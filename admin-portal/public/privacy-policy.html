Privacy Policy — Juvi

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Juvi is the mobile app for a college that runs on the Juvion platform. Your college operates the app for its students and faculty; Juvion provides the platform. This policy explains what the Juvi app collects, why, and the choices you have. It does not cover the college's own academic and financial records, which are governed by the college.

What we collect

  • Account and sign-in data. The name and identifiers your college already holds for you (roll number or employee code), and the device sessions created when you sign in.
  • Device push registration. A per-device token so the app can receive notifications, and its status while your account exists.
  • Notification activity. Which notices were sent to you, whether they were delivered, opened or acknowledged.
  • App usage events. Server-side events recording which notices you saw and acknowledged, used to operate the service.
  • Profile photo. If you upload one, it is stored on your college person record, not kept only on the device.

How we use it

  • Sign you in and show your timetable, teaching, dues and academics.
  • Deliver notices and notifications, and show their reach and acknowledgements.
  • Keep the service secure and diagnose crashes.

Sharing

Notifications are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging and crash reports are collected by Firebase Crashlytics, both Google services, and the app is distributed through Google Play. We do not sell your data. Notification content is not placed in the push payload's title or body for confidential notices.

Retention

Your Juvi data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your Juvi account removes the data described on the deletion page below. Your college's own records (person, student or faculty records, fees and the rest) are retained by the college under its record-keeping obligations and are not deleted by removing your Juvi account.

Your choices

  • Control notification tiers, mute, and quiet hours in the app's Settings.
  • Ask for your Juvi account to be deleted — see the deletion page.

Account deletion

You can delete your Juvi account from inside the app (immediately) or by requesting deletion on the web: Delete your Juvi account. A web request takes effect after a 7-day grace period, which gives you time to cancel a request you did not make.

Children

Juvi is for the enrolled students and staff of the college and is not directed at children outside that roll.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@juvion.in.
