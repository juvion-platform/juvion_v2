Delete your Juvi account

Juvi is the student and faculty app for your college. Use this page to ask for your Juvi account to be deleted — you do not need the app installed, and you do not need to sign in.

Your account is not removed immediately. Submitting this form records the request and the confirmation that follows gives the exact date your account will be removed. Until that date you can cancel the request by signing in to the app, or by asking your college office.

If it was not you who asked, sign in to Juvi as soon as you can — signing in or changing your password cancels a pending request.

What is deleted

  • Your Juvi account itself: its profile, its settings and notification preferences.
  • Your signed-in devices, which are signed out everywhere.
  • Your membership of Juvi spaces and your notification history.

What is not deleted

  • Your college's own records. Your admission, academic, attendance and fee records belong to the college and are not touched by this — deleting your Juvi account does not remove you from the college.
  • Notices and messages you published to others, which stay with the people who received them.

Getting access back

If you delete your Juvi account by mistake, your college can set it up again for you. That creates a fresh account with a new temporary password, and it does not restore what was deleted — so contact your college office instead if you only want to stop the deletion.

Juvi is developed by Juvion. This page is for account deletion requests only.
